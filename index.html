ABOUT ME

Learning continuously.
Predicting efficiently.

I am a third-year undergraduate at the School of Remote Sensing and Information Engineering, Wuhan University, majoring in Spatial Information and Digital Technology in the experimental program.

My research interests lie in world models, parameter-efficient adaptation, and inference optimization. I am interested in how models accumulate knowledge across tasks and how useful predictions can be made with limited computation.

I conduct research with Prof. Mang Ye at Wuhan University. My research experience also includes an internship at Great Wall Motor and collaboration with Peking University and Lightwheel on world action models.
